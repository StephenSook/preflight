(a) No person or entity shall make or cause to be made any telephone solicitation to the telephone line of any residential, mobile or wireless subscriber in the state of Georgia by whom notice has been given to the Commission in accordance with its Rule 515-14-1-.04 of said subscriber's objection to receiving same. (b) Any person who or entity that makes a telephone solicitation to the telephone line of any residential, mobile or wireless subscriber in this jurisdiction shall begin the call by clearly stating the identity of the person or entity on whose behalf the call is being made. (c) No person who or entity that makes a telephone solicitation to the telephone line of a residential, mobile or wireless subscriber in this state shall knowingly utilize any method to block or otherwise circumvent said subscriber's use of a caller identification service. (d) Any person who or entity that knowingly violates O.C.G.A. Section 46-5-27(c) by making a telephone solicitation to a residential, mobile or wireless telephone subscriber that is listed on the Commission's No Call Database shall be subject to the penalties set forth in O.C.G.A. Sections 46-5-27(h) and (i) .
